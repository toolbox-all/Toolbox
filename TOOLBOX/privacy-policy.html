Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

TOOLBOX tools run in your browser. The files, images, PDFs and text you use with our tools are not uploaded to our servers, and we don’t store them.

What stays on your device

  • Images, PDFs and text you add to a tool are processed on your own device.
  • Nothing you type or choose in a tool is sent to us.
  • When you close or reload the page, that data is gone.

Third-party services

To work properly, our pages load some resources from other providers. Their servers can see your IP address and browser details when your browser requests them.

  • Google Fonts for the typefaces used on the site.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) for the QR code, PDF merging and splitting, and PDF to image libraries.

Cookies and tracking

TOOLBOX does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If this changes, we will update this page before it happens.

Hosting logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic server logs, such as IP address, time and page requested, for security and reliability.

Children

TOOLBOX does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email your-email@example.com.
