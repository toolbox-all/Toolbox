PDF Splitter

Extract the pages you need, split a PDF into parts, or save every page as its own file. Everything happens in your browser.

Examples

  • Extract with “1-3, 7” gives one new PDF with pages 1, 2, 3 and 7.
  • Split ranges with “1-3, 7” gives two PDFs: one with pages 1-3 and one with page 7.
  • Password-protected PDFs can’t be split. Your file stays in your browser.
