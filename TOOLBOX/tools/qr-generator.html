QR Code Generator

Turn a link, phone number or any text into a QR code. Pick your colors and download it as a PNG.

Keep strong contrast between the two colors, with the QR darker than the background, so phones can scan it.

Type something to create your QR code.

Good to know

  • Your text is turned into a QR code in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Always scan the code with your phone before you print it.
  • Higher error correction makes the code denser but still readable if slightly damaged.
