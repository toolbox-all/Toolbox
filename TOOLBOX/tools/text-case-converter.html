Text Case Converter

Paste your text and switch it to any case with one click. Made a mistake? Press Undo.

Good to know

  • camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case and kebab-case turn your whole text into one name, which is handy for code and file names.
  • Tamil and other scripts without upper and lower case stay the same.
  • Your text stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
